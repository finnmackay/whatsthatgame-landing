How it works

Up and running in minutes

No installation, no hardware, no training required.

1
Sign up your venue

Create a business account and tell us about your venue — what type it is, your typical crowd size, and what kind of events you run.

2
Get your custom game pack

We curate a collection of games suited to your venue and guests — filtered by age, player count, equipment, and vibe. You can also browse and add your own.

3
Share with your guests

Print a QR code for your tables, share a link on your social channels, or hand your staff a tablet. Guests browse, pick a game, and start playing immediately.

4
Watch engagement grow

See which games your guests love most, track event attendance, and keep updating your collection as new games get added to the platform.

Business features

Everything an event organiser needs

Built for people who run events, not just play games.

📋
Curated Game Packs
Pre-built collections for specific events — pub quiz warm-ups, freshers week, hostel socials, and more.
🔗
QR Code Access
Generate a QR code for your venue. Guests scan it and land directly on your curated game list — no app download needed.
🎯
Smart Filters
Filter by player count, duration, age rating, and equipment. Always find the right game for the right moment.
🎲
Random Game Picker
Can't agree on what to play? Hit the dice button and let the app decide. Works great for large groups.
🛡️
Age-Safe Collections
For schools and under-18 events, filter to age-appropriate games only. Every game has a verified age rating.
📱
No App Required for Guests
Guests access games through a web link — no download, no sign-up, no friction. Just play.
