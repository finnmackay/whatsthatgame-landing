Features

Everything you need to find the perfect game

Whether you're hosting a house party or looking for something new to play with friends, we've got you.

🃏

Swipe to Discover

Tinder-style game discovery. Swipe right on games you want to save, left to skip. Filter by type, player count, and vibe before you start.

🔍

Powerful Search

Filter by number of players, how long you've got, age rating, equipment you have on hand, and game type. Find the right game for right now.

➕

Share Your Favourites

Got a game the world needs to know about? Add it to the platform. Include the full rules, setup, and what makes it great.

❤️

Build Your Wishlist

Save games you want to play. Build a wishlist for your next game night and share it with friends.

⬆️

Community Voting

The best games rise to the top. Upvote your favourites and see what the community is loving right now.

🎲

Can't Decide? Roll the Dice

Hit the random button and let the app pick for you. Perfect for when the group can't agree — just play whatever comes up.

How it works

From "what should we play?" to playing in minutes

Three simple steps to your next game night.

1
Tell us what you've got

Set your player count, how long you want to play, and what equipment you have. We'll narrow it down.

2
Swipe through your options

Browse game cards, swipe right on ones that catch your eye, or roll the dice for a random pick.

3
Read the rules and play

Every game comes with full setup instructions, rules, and the objective — everything you need to start immediately.
